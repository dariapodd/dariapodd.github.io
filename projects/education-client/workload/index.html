Data sources

There was little documentation of the role’s workload, so the tasks and their timing were collected from what coordinators and departments already used:

  • Internal documents
  • Checklists
  • Guidelines
  • Department onboarding materials
  • Personal trackers

Modelled data. Each project follows the same lifecycle: a baseline that peaks mid-cycle, with spikes at kick-off, the materials deadline, the mid-cycle review, the review window and results. At most 10 items per project per day; weekends carry a fraction of the load. Days over 15 items in total are marked busy.
